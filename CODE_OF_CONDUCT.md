# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Dossiero to be a welcoming, professional project. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept
constructive feedback, and focus disagreement on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Privacy matters here

Dossiero handles sensitive personal information. Never post real dossier contents, databases,
credentials, media, identifying screenshots, or private correspondence in public project spaces.
Use fictional or thoroughly anonymized examples.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Reports should be made
through the repository's GitHub Issues without including private or identifying information.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible.
