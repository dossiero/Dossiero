## What is Dossiero?

Dossiero is a desktop app for keeping a **dossier on each person you care about**: a contact, a client, a character in your novel, a subject of research. Each person gets their own profile with custom tabs for notes, photos and files, saved links, a map, and a graph of how they connect to other people.

Everything is stored **locally on your machine**. There is no account to create and no cloud to trust.

Good fit for:

- **Researchers and journalists** keeping track of people, sources and connections
- **Writers** building character and cast notes
- **Anyone** who wants a personal CRM that doesn't send their data anywhere

## What you can do

- **Build profiles for people.** Pin the important ones, archive the ones you're done with, restore or delete them for good.
- **Organize with custom tabs.** Add text, media, link, relationship, and map tabs, and arrange each profile the way you think.
- **See how people connect.** Draw relationship graphs. Your layout is remembered per view.
- **Write rich notes.** Format your notes and search across everything in your dossiers.
- **Pin things on a map.** Use map layers and annotations, and import or export them.
- **Keep your files organized.** Photos and documents go into a managed media vault, separate per person.
- **Back up in one click.** Export everything to a single ZIP file.

## Privacy

Privacy is the point of this app.
<br />
There's no account, no sign-up and no telemetry, and your database and files live in a folder on your own computer.
